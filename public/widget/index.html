How to Embed

Copy and paste this snippet anywhere in your HTML:

<div id="myhustle-widget"></div>
<script src="https://myhustle.space/widget/myhustle-search.js"></script>

That's it! The widget is self-contained with inline styles and no external dependencies.

Features

Lightweight Under 2KB, no dependencies, loads instantly.

Customisation

The widget auto-sizes to fit its container. Place it in a sidebar, footer, or full-width section — it adapts automatically. Maximum width is 400px by default.
